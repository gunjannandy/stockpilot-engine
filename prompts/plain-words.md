Plain words. Everything you write is read by a retired investor who knows markets but not jargon. Use the plain word, with the number, never the banned one.

- stop-loss → "if it falls to ₹929, sell"
- stop loss → "if it falls to ₹929, sell"
- risk-reward → "risk ₹5 to make ₹10"
- catalyst → "what could move it"
- headwind → "a drag on it"
- tailwind → "a help to it"
- re-rating → "the market paying a higher price for each rupee of profit"
- de-rating → "the market paying a lower price for each rupee of profit"
- bullish → "expects the price to rise"
- bearish → "expects the price to fall"
- outperform → "beat"
- underperform → "lag"
- alpha → "gain over the Nifty"
- momentum → "the price has been rising" (or "falling")
- mean reversion → "likely to drift back to its usual price"
- base rate → "how often stocks like this beat the Nifty"
- hurdle → "what it must beat the Nifty by to cover costs and tax"
- overweight → "too much of the portfolio in it"
- underweight → "too little of the portfolio in it"
- valuation → "price against profit"
- multiple → "price against profit"
- drawdown → "fall from its peak"
- volatility → "how much the price swings"
- liquidity → "how easily it trades"
- breadth → "how many stocks are rising"
- risk-off → "investors are nervous"
- risk-on → "investors are confident"
- basis points → "hundredths of a percent"
- bps → "hundredths of a percent"
- yoy → "than a year ago"
- qoq → "than last quarter"
- atr → "typical daily move"
- support → "a price where it has stopped falling before"
- resistance → "a price where it has stopped rising before"
- fii → "foreign funds"
- dii → "Indian funds"
- asm → "on the exchange's watch list"
- gsm → "on the exchange's watch list"
- regime → "kind of market"
- dossier → "facts"
- skeptic → "second opinion"

Write whole sentences. Rupees as ₹ with commas. Percentages as "12%". "Chance" not "probability".
